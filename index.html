Privacy Policy - AppleRush

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. No Accounts

AppleRush does not require login. Just download and play.

2. Ads

This game is free and supported by Interstitial and Rewarded Ads. Ad partners: Google AdMob, AppLovin.

3. Data We Collect

We only collect anonymous device info, crash reports, and advertising ID for ads. We do NOT collect name, email, or location.

4. Data Deletion Request

AppleRush respects your privacy. You can request deletion of your advertising ID and associated ad data at any time.

How to request deletion:
1. Send an email to: your-games@yemagames.com
2. Subject: "Delete My Data - AppleRush"
3. Include your Advertising ID if possible (we will delete all associated data even without it)

What data is deleted: We and our ad partners (Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Meta Audience Network) will delete your advertising ID, device info, and crash logs. Data is deleted within 30 days.

Data retention: Ad data is automatically deleted within 90 days. We do not keep personal data.
